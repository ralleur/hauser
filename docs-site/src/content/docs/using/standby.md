A wall panel spends most of the day doing nothing. Standby is that resting state. It should be readable from across the room and quiet until touched.

![The standby screen: a large clock and date, temperatures below, the week along the bottom, reminders on the right, the shopping list on the left, over a faint street map.](/media/lockscreen-1100.webp)

## What it shows

- The **clock** and the date.
- **Outdoor and indoor temperature** with a trend.
- The **week strip** with upcoming appointments. It can be switched off under **Appearance → Ambient & standby → Calendar in standby**.
- **Reminders** as coloured notes and the **shopping list**.
- **Weather** passing behind the clock: rain, snow, cloud. Switch **Weather in standby** under Ambient & standby.
- An optional **street map** of your own town. See [OpenStreetMap](/docs/integrations/openstreetmap/).
- One line for **moments**: a birthday, the first snow. See [Presence and moments](/docs/using/presence-and-moments/).

Touching the calendar or the notes opens that screen. Touching anything else opens the home screen. The opening is a soft ring from your finger, not a cut.

## When it starts

**Settings → Appearance → Ambient & standby**:

- **Time until standby** sets the idle delay, or switches standby off for this device.
- **Start standby** switches now. The standby button in the title bar does the same.

## Commute view

The car button switches between the normal standby screen and your configured
commute. A manual choice is remembered on this device when you visit a room
and return, and after restarting the app. Long departure countdowns use hours
and minutes. Configure office days and arrival times under **Commute**.

## Weather source

**Settings → Appearance → Ambient & standby → Custom weather source & sensors**
lets you select a Home Assistant weather entity. Optional temperature, rain,
wind and humidity entities replace the current readings. Leave a field empty
to use Open-Meteo or that weather entity's own values. See
[weather sources](/docs/integrations/open-meteo/) for the details.

## Deep night

Between 22:00 and 06:00 the screen dims to an eye-friendly deep night. The map disappears. Switch it under **Deep night**.

## Presence

A motion detector can wake the panel from standby. When every resident is away, standby stays black. The first person home is greeted. All three are off by default. See [Presence and moments](/docs/using/presence-and-moments/).

## Ambient light

**Brightness follows the room** in the stage's layout menu lets a light sensor in the room or in the panel dim the interface along with the room.

## Hidden gestures

- Long-press the clock for a large date with seconds.
- Tap the logo three times for a diagnostics view: frame rate, connection age, snapshot age, version. The same gesture reveals the hidden settings sections.

## AI text on the lock screen

An optional line generated by a language model. It is **off by default** and needs a separately running model service. Without one the switch does nothing useful. The built-in lines about weather and appointments need no AI.
