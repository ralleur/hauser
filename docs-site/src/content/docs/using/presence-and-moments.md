## Residents

Residents are mapped to Home Assistant persons. They are imported on first start and appear on the pinboard in Notes. Tapping a name renames the person. Each person has a colour that follows their notes and their calendar.

## Presence

Three switches under **Settings → Appearance → Ambient & standby**, all off by default:

- **Wake on motion.** A sensor from *Windows & motion* brings the panel out of standby.
- **Dark when nobody is home.** While every assigned resident is away, standby stays black.
- **Personal greeting.** Whoever comes home first is greeted in standby, with their own notes on top.

## Moments

Small celebrations that do not need a setting for every case:

- **Birthdays** from the family calendar show a quiet morning confetti and a line in standby.
- **Special days** such as Christmas, New Year's Eve and Easter can be selected under **Settings → Content → Calendar & Reminders → Special days for moments**.
- **The first snow** of the season is noticed once.
