## In a room

The thermostat is a tile the size of a device. It shows the target and the current temperature and the mode as its symbol.

- **Tap** opens the thermostat sheet: current temperature, setpoint with minus and plus, and every mode the device reports. Fan, preset and swing appear when the device has them. The scale follows the device's own limits.
- **Long press** opens the tile's settings: **Controls in the surface** brings the full thermostat card back into the control surface, **Tile shows** picks target, current or both, and **Step size** sets 0.5° or 1° per tap.

Climate changes get the same five-second [Undo](/hauser/docs/using/scenes/) as scenes.

## The whole house

The climate pill in the title bar and in the phone's quick-action row sets every thermostat at once. Two round buttons, one dominant target value, the measured current value behind a fine divider.

A **long press on the reading area** of the pill opens the settings that decide what it controls. This works on the panel and on the phone, from any screen.

## Holiday mode

If the household configuration binds a vacation switch, the phone's quick actions carry a holiday-mode button next to the climate pill. The quick action next to the temperature can also carry any other device.

## Temperature on tiles

A room tile can show its current temperature. Room and outdoor temperature also appear on the [standby screen](/hauser/docs/using/standby/), with a rising or falling trend.
