Hauser is a calm, visual frontend for [Home Assistant](https://www.home-assistant.io/). It is made for a wall panel, a tablet and the phones of the people who live in the home.

Home Assistant stays the admin tool. Hauser is the everyday surface: one picture of each room, the lights and heating in that room, and the small things a household needs during the day.

![The Hauser home screen by day: a control panel on the left, a sunlit illustration of the living room on the right.](/hauser/media/hero-home-1100.webp)
