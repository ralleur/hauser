Your Apple Home. Familiar rooms on iPhone and iPad.

Hauser is a native iPhone and iPad app that can connect directly to Apple Home. Your rooms and supported devices come from your existing home. There is no Hauser server to install and no Home Assistant requirement for this connection.

Open the demo ↗Set up →

Free. Open source. Public beta.

Everyday control, room by room

Open the living room, choose a scene or change a light. Hauser groups the room controls together. The native app includes room pictures; the iPad also offers image control. Supported devices and features depend on your Apple Home connection. The web demo is a separate preview and does not connect to Apple Home.

What you need

Use an iPhone or iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later, Apple’s TestFlight app and an existing Apple Home with supported devices. Join the Hauser beta, open the app and choose Apple Home. Allow Home access when prompted. The setup guide explains the supported connection paths.

Free, open source and in public beta

Hauser does not charge for the app or require a Hauser account or subscription for everyday control. This is a personal project without guaranteed support. TestFlight builds expire and need updates. Optional image generation and other external integrations have their own requirements and costs.

Already using Home Assistant?

The same native app can instead pair with a Hauser server connected to Home Assistant. This is a different connection path, with different available devices and features. Apple Home does not automatically gain the integrations of a Home Assistant server.
