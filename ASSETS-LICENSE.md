## Provenance

The room illustrations and background artwork are **AI-generated**. They were
produced for this project and depict stylized interiors; they are not
photographs and are not stock imagery.

This is stated explicitly because provenance matters when you reuse assets: if
your project or jurisdiction has requirements around AI-generated material, or
if you need assets with a documented human-authored chain of title, these are
not suitable and you should replace them.
