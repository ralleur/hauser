The interface speaks **German, English, French, Italian, Portuguese and Polish**.

- It follows the browser language unless you choose one under **Settings → Appearance → Interface language**.
- Switching happens without a reload. The panel keeps its connection.
- Dates, times and numbers follow the chosen language.
- Room, device and scene names are **not** translated. They come from your configuration.

## Honest caveats

German and English are first-hand. French, Italian, Portuguese and Polish were written carefully but not reviewed by native speakers. Corrections are welcome, see the [open translation issues](https://github.com/ralleur/hauser/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Atranslation).

Adding a language means adding one JSON file. See [Translations](/hauser/docs/developers/translations/).
