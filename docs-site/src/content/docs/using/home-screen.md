The home screen is where a panel spends its active time. It has two parts.

- **The stage** is the large room picture. It follows daylight, the weather and the lights in the room.
- **The control surface** holds the room's scenes and devices. On the panel it sits beside the stage.

![The home screen after dark: identical layout on dark surfaces, the room lit only by its own lamps.](/hauser/media/home-dark-1100.webp)

## Three views

Press and hold a free area of the stage, or swipe left on it, to open the
room configuration. The view switch at the top centre chooses between:

- **Full screen.** One room picture, the room picker at the top of the control surface.
- **All rooms.** Every room as a tile with picture, name and light status. The control surface belongs to the selected room and carries its name.
- **Image control.** Only the room picture. The control surface stays away, and the room is used through its devices in the picture: tap a lamp where it stands to switch it, hold it for its detail. Swipe to change rooms. A double tap on an empty spot shows the zones and names for a moment. Devices get their spot in **Assign devices** (see [room pictures](/hauser/docs/using/room-images/#devices-in-the-picture)).

![Image control in the evening: the living room without a control surface, its lamps and blinds shown in the picture after a double tap.](/hauser/media/picture-control-1100.webp)

## Swipe the controls away

Swipe left on the control surface and it flies out of the picture. The stage takes the full width. The next touch brings it back. A tap on a tile also selects that room.

To let it go by itself, set **Hide control surface** to **Automatic** in the room configuration and choose the time without touch, from 5 seconds to 2 minutes.

## Room configuration

The room configuration holds the layout as well. Every setting takes effect at once:

- the view, at the top centre
- the width of the control area: drag the pill next to the control surface
- rooms per page in Full screen, rooms per row and rows at once in All rooms, under the rooms
- hiding the control surface automatically, a second control surface at the bottom with a fixed room of its own, and **Reset to default**, in the side column

## Title bar

The title bar carries the connection state, the [edit or use mode](/hauser/docs/using/edit-and-use-mode/) mark in the middle, the appearance button, the central climate pill, a question mark and the standby button. The question mark opens a short sheet to [report a problem or make a suggestion](/hauser/docs/reference/faq/#how-do-i-report-a-problem-or-make-a-suggestion).

## Idea behind it

The room should stay visible. Controls stay narrow, and when you want the picture alone, you swipe them away.
